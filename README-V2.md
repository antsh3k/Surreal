# 🧠 Surreal: Guided Mental Maps for Humans & Machines

### **Minimalist Discovery Engine for Context-Rich Exploration**

> **Core Philosophy:** Discovery over Configuration | Minimal Input, Maximum Insight | Intelligence that Adapts to You

---

## 🎯 What is Surreal?

Surreal transforms any topic into an **interactive mental map** that learns your preferences while you explore. Unlike traditional knowledge graphs, Surreal starts neutral and develops **opinions** about what will interest you based on your exploration patterns.

**For Humans:** A guided research companion that minimizes cognitive load
**For Machines:** A context management system that captures human reasoning patterns
**For Teams:** A shared mental model that evolves with collective intelligence

---

## 🎨 The Minimalist Experience

### Visual Language
- **Clean Base**: White background, black text, subtle gray borders
- **Interactive Hints**: Dashed borders mean "click to expand"
- **Learned Preferences**: 
  - Subtle green tint = "System thinks you'll like this"
  - Subtle orange tint = "Uncertain relevance"
  - Neutral gray = "Learning your preferences"

### Two-Action Design
1. **Left Click (Epistemic)**: Expand knowledge, create new concepts
2. **Right Click (Pragmatic)**: Generate content, create multimedia

## 🚀 Three Core Use Cases

### 1. **Research Assistant** 
*"Learn Active Inference in AI"*

You input a topic → System creates 4-5 neutral concept nodes → You click what interests you → System expands and learns your preferences → Generates diagrams, finds papers, suggests next areas → Becomes your personalized research companion

**Value**: Eliminates research paralysis, creates guided discovery paths

### 2. **Code Understanding**
*"How does authentication work in this Next.js app?"*

System scans your codebase → Creates nodes for key concepts → You explore specific implementations → Right-click to generate diagrams → Visual flow of your actual code → Onboard new team members instantly

**Value**: Turns complex codebases into navigable mental models

### 3. **Creative Exploration**
*"History of Jazz Music"*

Start with broad concepts → Dive into specific eras → Right-click to generate playlists → YouTube videos become expandable nodes → Each song spawns artist/era connections → Build comprehensive understanding through multimedia

**Value**: Makes learning feel like play, not work

## 🧬 The Core Innovation: Preference Learning

Traditional tools show you everything. Surreal learns what matters to **you**.

### How It Works
1. **Neutral Start**: All concepts begin with equal weight
2. **Implicit Feedback**: Your clicks reveal interests and priorities  
3. **Adaptive Hints**: System highlights concepts you're likely to explore
4. **Personalized Paths**: Future explorations adapt to your discovered preferences

### Why This Matters
- **For Researchers**: Focus on relevant literature, skip noise
- **For Students**: Personalized learning paths based on natural curiosity
- **For Professionals**: Quickly navigate complex domains with smart guidance
- **For AI Systems**: Capture human reasoning patterns for context management
